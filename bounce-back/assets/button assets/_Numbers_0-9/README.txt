DIGITAL GRAFFITI ASSET PACK -- Numbers 01 --
================================================================================

Thank you for purchasing / downloading this pack!

All assets in this folder are hand-drawn digitally respecting real graffiti paint technique without use of AI or other graffiti reference.

These assets are ready for plug-and-play use in your commercial and non-commercial 
games, 3D scenes, mods, or digital environments.

20 PNG Assets (Transparent Background / Alpha Channel):

- 0_Numbers_Black     : 10 png files black color
- 1_Numbers_White       : 10 png files red color
- Atlases (2x)
